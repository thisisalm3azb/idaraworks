# Installing your company app

Give this to a customer as it is. It assumes nothing technical and it never
promises a step their device cannot do.

Your company app is a real application: it gets its own icon, opens in its own
window, and goes straight into your workspace. It updates itself — there is
nothing to download and no version to keep track of.

**You will need:** your company's app address, and your usual IdaraWorks login.
Find the address in **Settings → Company app**, or ask whoever set up your
workspace.

---

## Windows — Chrome or Edge

1. Open your company app address in Chrome or Edge.
2. Sign in.
3. Press **Install app** in the top bar.
   *(If you do not see it: click the ⋮ menu, then **Cast, save and share → Install
   this page as an app**, or the small install icon at the right of the address
   bar.)*
4. Choose **Install**.

It now appears in your Start menu and can be pinned to the taskbar like any
other program.

---

## Mac — Safari

Safari does not show an install button. Use the menu bar instead.

1. Open your company app address in Safari.
2. Sign in.
3. In the menu bar choose **File → Add to Dock**.
4. Confirm the name and choose **Add**.

It now appears in your Dock and in Launchpad.

> Using Chrome or Edge on a Mac? Follow the Windows steps above — the **Install
> app** button works there.

---

## iPhone and iPad

There is no install button on iPhone or iPad. Apple provides one route, and this
is it.

1. Open your company app address in **Safari**.
2. Sign in.
3. Tap the **Share** button (the square with an arrow pointing up).
4. Scroll down and tap **Add to Home Screen**.
5. Tap **Add**.

The app appears on your home screen with your company's icon.

> On iOS 16.4 and later this also works from Chrome, Edge and Firefox. On older
> iPhones it must be Safari.

---

## Android — Chrome

1. Open your company app address in Chrome.
2. Sign in.
3. Tap **Install app** in the top bar.
   *(Or the ⋮ menu, then **Add to Home screen**.)*
4. Tap **Install**.

---

## Desktop Firefox

Firefox cannot install web apps. That is a Firefox limitation and nothing to do
with your workspace.

You have two options:

- **Install it in Chrome, Edge or Safari instead**, following the steps above.
- **Keep using it in Firefox as a normal website** and bookmark the address.
  Everything works; it simply lives in a browser tab rather than its own window.

---

## After it is installed

**It opens straight into your workspace.** No marketing page, no hunting for a
link.

**If you are signed out**, you will see your company's sign-in page. Sign in and
you land exactly where you were going.

**It updates by itself.** When we release an improvement you get it the next time
you open the app.

**It needs a connection.** If you are offline the app says so on a clear screen.
It deliberately does not keep your business records on the device — see the note
below.

---

## Two questions people always ask

**"Can I use it offline?"**
No, and that is on purpose. Your invoices, reports and customer details are not
stored on the phone or laptop. If a device is lost or shared, there is nothing on
it to find. When you reconnect, everything is there.

**"Will it ever say something saved when it did not?"**
No. The app never accepts a change while disconnected. If it tells you something
was saved, it reached our servers.

---

## If something goes wrong

| What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| No **Install app** button on Windows or Android | You may already have it installed — check your Start menu or home screen. Otherwise use the browser menu route above. |
| The app opens the IdaraWorks home page instead of your workspace | The address is wrong. Copy it again from **Settings → Company app**. |
| "You do not have access to this workspace" | Your membership was removed or you signed in with a different account. Contact whoever administers your workspace. |
| The icon is grey or generic | Your workspace has not set an app icon yet. An administrator can do that in **Settings → Company app**. |

Anything else: contact IdaraWorks support with your company name and what you
saw. You do not need to reinstall to fix problems — the app is always running the
current version.
